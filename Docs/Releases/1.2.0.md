**Gaea is alpha.** MES and Gaea are still changing as more plugins arrive, so read each release's notes before you
update.

What's new in 1.2.0:

- **Jobs are charged for their size.** A lane can declare how its memory grows with a job: an `ActivationScaling` line
  over output pixels. Gaea now admits and charges each job at that line's value for the job's size, and only for
  while it runs, instead of one flat figure for every job on the lane.
  - A small job no longer pays for the largest, and a large one is charged what it needs, so the governor evicts or
    refuses correctly.
  - A lane that scales doesn't learn a single "measured peak" from its largest job.
  - Lanes without a scaling line are charged as before.
- **FLUX.2 Klein and FLUX 3 Klein at 2048² and 4096².** gaea-plugin-flux2-klein 0.3.0 and gaea-plugin-flux3-klein
  0.2.0 draw a smaller image, then FlowUpscaler (TensorForger, Unlicense) doubles its latents once or twice before
  the decode. The result is the picture the smaller draw made, with finer detail, and effective reports it as
  `x-klein.upscaled` or `x-flux3.upscaled`.
  - **FLUX.2 Klein:** about 12 s for 2048² and 32 s for 4096² (bf16), against 7 s for 1024².
  - **FLUX 3 Klein:** draws natively up to 2048² as before, and upscales past it (about 41 s for 4096²).
  - **Memory:** a 4096² Klein 4B job is charged about 41.7 GB while it runs, and a 1024² one 25.9 GB. Both plugins need
    Gaea 1.2.0.
