**Gaea is alpha.** MES and Gaea are still changing as more plugins arrive, so read each release's notes before you
update.

What's new in 1.9.2: Gaea has an icon.

- **The icon is a picture Gaea made of itself.** A sphere of dots in the site's colours, generated with Z-Image Turbo
  through `image.generate` and picked from 38 candidates. Its prompt and seed are in the repo
  (`Apps/Gaea/Icon/README.md`). The Dock, the app switcher and Finder showed a blank placeholder before.

Nothing else changes, for the server or for the plugins already installed.
