**Gaea is alpha.** MES and Gaea are still changing as more plugins arrive, so read each release's notes before you
update.

What's new in 1.9.3: a faster draft for `image.generate`.

- **Draft pictures come from Klein 4B's one-step model.** `image.generate` at quality `draft` now runs `klein-4b-rdm`:
  EPFL VITA's one-step fine-tune of FLUX.2 Klein 4B (Apache-2.0), on Klein's own encoder and VAE. It's new in
  [gaea-plugin-flux2-klein 0.4.0](https://github.com/xocialize-code/gaea-plugin-flux2-klein). In TierBench through
  Gaea at 1024²:
  - it took 2.8 s against the 4-step model's 6.5 s;
  - its SigLIP2 quality was the same (0.903 / 0.862 against 0.903 / 0.866);
  - its prompt-keyword recall on the hard prompts was 0.94 against 0.89 (AB-R-1056).

  It spells words in the picture a little less reliably, and it ignores negative prompts. A job that names `klein-4b`,
  or wants the 4-step model's spelling, still gets it.
- **Update the Klein plugin to get it.** Run `gaea plugins update com.xocialize.gaea.flux2-klein`, or wait for the
  check. The plugin downloads `mlx-community/FLUX.2-klein-4B-1step-RDM-bf16` (about 16 GB: RDM's DiT and its own copy
  of Klein's encoder and VAE) the first time a draft job runs. Until then, draft stays on Klein 4B (4 steps).
- **Prompt enhancement** rewrites the new model's prompts with the Klein profile, from
  [gaea-plugin-prompt-enhance 0.1.3](https://github.com/xocialize-code/gaea-plugin-prompt-enhance) on.

Standard and best are unchanged. pixel-agency-normalizer 0.4.1.
