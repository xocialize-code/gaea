## What it took

**gaea-plugin-ltx 0.1.0** has one model per LTX tier (VIDEO-EVALUATION §6.1), each with one lane and each offering
`video.generate`, `video.extend` and `video.retake`. Every tier streams its DiT from the granule tree:

| Model | `quality` | Generate size (conditioned) | Frames | Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ltx-2.5-compact24` | draft | 704×512 (512×288) | 121 | one stage, int8, int8 encoder |
| `ltx-2.5-balanced32` | draft, standard | 576×320 | 161 | one stage, int8, int8 encoder |
| `ltx-2.5-standard64` | standard, best | 1280×704 | 161 | two stages, int8 |
| `ltx-2.5-max128` | best | 1920×1088 | 481 | two stages, bf16 |

- **Grids.** The plugin snaps a request to the nearest 8k + 1 frames and /32 (or /64) size within the tier, with
  `duration_snapped`, `size_snapped` and `keyframe_moved`, so the package never clamps or snaps down on its own.
- **Inputs.** Every tier takes a first frame. Two-stage tiers also take a last frame, keyframes at times and a
  soundtrack, which sets the clip's length when no duration is asked for.
- **Extend** adds whole 8-frame steps. **Retake** widens its span to whole latent frames, with `span_snapped`, and
  remakes `picture`, `sound` or `both`.
- **The licence.** Each offer says `accepted_required` and `user_accept_required`, under
  `LicenseRef-LTX-2-Community`, with its notice.

Its in-process reference is `ltx-inproc`.

**Gaea 0.11.0:**
- **Disclosure** (Video §1.9): generate, extend and retake outputs carry `AI-generated by <model>` in the comment
  item, or `AI-generated in part by <model>` after the source's own comment for edits.
- **`audio: false`** leaves the track out. A generative output is retimed to the plugin's rate.
- **Kept frames** (§1.8, §3.2, §3.3): LTX decodes the whole clip from latents, so the frames an edit held came back
  at about 21 dB against the 128×96 test clip. Gaea now puts the source's own frames back outside a retake's span
  and before an extend's `overlap_s`, re-encoded in H.264 with the model's sound.
- **Edits' sources:**
  - **422 `input_invalid`:** a source off the offer's frame grid, or a sound retake on a silent one.
  - **422 `out_of_limits`:** a source past the offer's size or frames, a retake span past the clip, or an extend
    whose total is too long.
- **The operator's acceptance** (VID-6): an offer whose licence says `accepted_required` is opt-in
  (`ModelOffer.isOptIn`), even though MLXEngine counts LTX-2 Community as permissive.
- **GPP-1 §3.5:** a worker's `invalid_request` may name the MES refusal (`detail.mes_code`, `param`). The vendored
  MES schemas are v0.9.0's.

**GaeaPluginKit 0.9.1:** a lane the engine prepared skips the package's weight probe at run time. LTX's
`missingWeightSources` always reports its granule tree missing (AB-A-0240), so every run had been refused
`not_materialized` after a successful prepare.

## Found on the way

- **The package's weight probe can't see its granule tree.** `LTX2Configuration.missingWeightSources` sends a
  `granules` source down the transformer's check, so every 2.5 tier reports the granules missing with the tree on
  disk. The engine's prepare re-fetches what's "missing" and passes, then GaeaPluginKit's run-time probe refused the
  run. The kit now trusts a prepared lane (0.9.1). Asked of mlx-ltx as AB-A-0240.
- **int8 is not reproducible across processes.** At the same seed, two processes often agree byte for byte, but
  not always. In the first golden run, compact24's generate differed in its last four frames (42–44 dB), and the
  retake throughout (35–42 dB). In the second, those agreed and the last-frame and extend runs differed instead
  (within 50 and 32 dB). max128 (bf16) never differed. The int8 offers say `deterministic: false`, which VIDEO-015
  honours. The golden takes int8 pairs within 30 dB, and max128 must match byte for byte.
- **Kept frames needed Gaea.** The suite's VIDEO-009 and VIDEO-010 allow a 16/255 mean error on kept frames; the VAE
  round trip of the test clip was close to that. With the source's frames put back they are within 2.3/255.
- **LTX weights live on DEV_VOL1.** Gaea's default models root, MLXEngine's store, holds symbolic links to
  `models--mlx-community--ltx-2.5-mlx`, `-mlx-q8` and `models--xocialize--ltx-2.5-granules` there. The acceptance ran
  with `--models-root` on DEV_VOL1 directly.

## Not covered

- **Tier budgets** at 32, 64 and 128 GB (order 5), and sizes above 256×256 or long clips through Gaea.
- **LoRAs and IC-LoRAs** wait on typed adapters (AB-A-0197). Extending before a clip isn't supported (the package
  extends forward only). Prompt enhancement for video isn't wired (`prompt_enhance: on` warns).
- **Previews:** LTX sends no frames during a run (VIDEO-014 skips).
- **max128's storage floor** (bf16, 1 GB/s): DEV_VOL1 is fast enough, and a slow volume wasn't tried.
